    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, five stages, about 200 hours according to PowerPyx and GTABase. The order below is built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, then the open world with your partner, then activities, then collecting and money, and the 100% checklist last. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off.

    Missable trophies

    Six trophies need attention before the story moves past them. Everything else on the list can be earned after the credits, in free roam.

    Do this first: in Chapter 1, meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot at Jason’s house — the first spot for Positive Mental Attitude is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. Then start every stranger chain as soon as it unlocks.

      About this guide

      Leonida Trophy Ledger is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. There are no online trophies — every one is single-player and offline.

      Where the facts come from

      • Every trophy’s name, description and grade is shown as PlayStation publishes it, checked against the Exophase trophy list and the PSNProfiles trophy list, which agree on all 55.
      • The progress targets (for example 42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida) are PSN’s own counts, as listed by PSNProfiles.
      • The hidden flags (28 trophies) follow the hidden-trophy list published by EGamers, which matches the 28-hidden total on the other launch lists.
      • Each guide is written in our own words for this guide. Where a fact comes from another guide — GTABase or PowerPyx, as quoted by EGamers — the guide names it beside the trophy. Where no published guide had confirmed a method yet, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.

      How to use it

      • Tick a trophy when you earn it; the tick is saved in this browser.
      • For trophies with a PSN count, type your current number into the counter box — it saves too.
      • Use Export progress to save a backup file, and Import progress to restore it on another browser.
      • Turn on Hide story spoilers to keep story trophy names and descriptions hidden until you earn them.
      • The roadmap orders the work; the missables page lists the six trophies that need doing before the story passes them.

      Honesty note

      This guide was written on launch week, from the official list and the first published guides. A few trophies still have no confirmed method anywhere; their guides say so and give the description itself as the hint. Methods marked as reported by GTABase or PowerPyx are early reports and may change as more players finish the game.
